Question

What is the principle of conservation of momentum?

Tap to reveal answer touch_app
check_circle

Answer

The total momentum of an isolated system remains constant provided no external resultant force acts upon it.

Formula: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂
