Classification
--------------
1. Implementation bug: NO
   - One GGUF mmap, one embedder singleton, FAISS ~3 MB.
2. Configuration issue: PARTIAL / MINOR
   - FA off + full SWA waste ~200 MB; fixed in production defaults.
   - n_ctx and FA/SWA are NOT the dominant 2.7 GB Qwen→Gemma delta.
3. Unavoidable Gemma property: YES (dominant)
   - E4B reports ~7.52B parameters; Q4_K_M weights alone ~4.7 GB RSS.
   - llm-only Ready RSS 7371 MB already exceeds the 7 GB full-stack budget.
   - Plus ~0.75 GB KEmbed → full-stack Ready floor ≈ 7.9 GB even after FA/SWA.

Historical Qwen3-4B Ready ~5.4 GB with ~2.5 GB GGUF is consistent with a
smaller true-4B model; the ~2.7 GB regression is mostly larger Gemma weights
(+~2.2 GB file) plus Gemma runtime/KV anon overhead, not a double-load bug.

STEP 4 - Applied optimization
-----------------------------
Production Llama() now uses FLASH_ATTN=true, SWA_FULL=false (env-overridable).
Measured Ready ≈ 7889 MB (still above 7 GB target).
Did NOT cut n_ctx below 4096 (would break MAX_CONTEXT_TOKENS=3000 + MAX_TOKENS=512).
Did NOT replace Gemma / revert to Qwen.
