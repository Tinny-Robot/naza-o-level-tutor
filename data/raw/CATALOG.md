# O-Level Textbook Library

This folder contains freely distributed or openly licensed study materials suitable for senior secondary study.

## Folder Map

- `SS1/`: Grade 10 equivalents for Mathematics and Physical Sciences, including teacher guides.
- `SS2/`: Grade 11 equivalents for Mathematics and Physical Sciences, including teacher guides.
- `SS3/`: Grade 12 equivalents for Mathematics and Physical Sciences, including a teacher guide.
- `Reference/NIOS/`: Complete senior-secondary Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry books, plus Physics and Chemistry laboratory manuals.
- `Reference/NCERT/`: Official Class 10 to Class 12 Mathematics, English, Physics, and Chemistry books, extracted into chapter PDFs.

## Sources and Licenses

### Siyavula

- Source: Siyavula Open Textbooks
- Coverage: Mathematics and Physical Sciences
- Level mapping: Grade 10 to SS1, Grade 11 to SS2, Grade 12 to SS3
- License: Creative Commons licenses shown inside each book, commonly CC BY-ND

### NIOS

- Source: National Institute of Open Schooling senior-secondary course material
- Coverage: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and laboratory work
- Format: Complete books and laboratory manuals

### NCERT

- Source: National Council of Educational Research and Training official textbook downloads
- Coverage: Class 10 Mathematics and English, plus Class 11 and Class 12 Mathematics, English, Physics, and Chemistry
- Format: Chapter-level PDFs extracted from official textbook archives

## Important Note

Commercial Nigerian textbooks such as the Essential series are not included because unauthorized PDF copies would infringe the publishers' rights. These open resources cover much of the same senior-secondary material, but their topic order differs from the Nigerian curriculum. Use a current Nigerian SS1 to SS3 scheme of work to sequence the chapters.
