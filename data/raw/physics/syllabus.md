# WAEC Physics Syllabus

## 1. Mechanics
- Units and Dimensions: Fundamental and derived units, dimensional analysis and applications.
- Kinematics: Linear motion, speed, velocity, acceleration, equations of motion, projectile motion, time of flight, range, maximum height.
- Dynamics: Newton's laws of motion, inertia, momentum, impulse, conservation of linear momentum, elastic and inelastic collisions.
- Statics: Forces, coplanar forces, moment of a force, couples, conditions of equilibrium, center of gravity.
- Work, Energy, and Power: Work done by constant and variable forces, kinetic and potential energy, conservation of energy, power.
- Circular Motion: Angular velocity, centripetal acceleration and force, applications (banking of roads, centrifuge).
- Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM): Period, frequency, amplitude, equations of SHM, simple pendulum, mass-spring system, energy transformations in SHM.
- Hydrostatics and Properties of Matter: Density, relative density, pressure in liquids, Archimedes' principle, law of floatation, elasticity, Hooke's law, Young's modulus.

## 2. Thermal Physics (Heat)
- Temperature and its Measurement: Thermometric properties, temperature scales (Celsius, Kelvin, Fahrenheit), liquid-in-glass, gas, resistance thermometers.
- Thermal Expansion: Linear, area, and volume expansivity of solids and liquids, anomalous expansion of water.
- Gas Laws: Boyle's law, Charles's law, Pressure law, ideal gas equation ($PV = nRT$).
- Heat Capacity: Specific heat capacity, determination by mixture method and electrical method, latent heat of fusion and vaporization.
- Heat Transfer: Conduction, convection, and radiation. Stefan-Boltzmann law.

## 3. Waves & Optics
- Wave Motion: Transverse and longitudinal waves, mathematical wave equation ($y = A \sin(\omega t - kx)$), reflection, refraction, diffraction, interference, and polarization.
- Sound Waves: Speed of sound, pitch, loudness, quality/timbre, resonance, vibration in pipes (open and closed) and stretched strings.
- Reflection of Light: Reflection at plane surfaces, spherical mirrors (concave and convex), mirror formula, magnification.
- Refraction of Light: Refractive index, Snell's law, critical angle, total internal reflection, prisms, lenses (thin converging and diverging lenses), lens formula, defects of lenses.
- Optical Instruments: Camera, simple and compound microscopes, astronomical telescope, human eye (defects and corrections).

## 4. Electricity & Magnetism
- Electrostatics: Coulomb's law, electric field, electric potential, capacitors and capacitance, combination of capacitors (series and parallel).
- Current Electricity: Ohm's law, electromotive force (e.m.f.), internal resistance of a cell, resistivity, electrical energy and power, series-parallel combination of resistors, Kirchhoff's laws.
- Magnetic Fields: Permanent and electromagnetism, magnetic field around current-carrying conductors, force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field.
- Electromagnetic Induction: Faraday's laws, Lenz's law, self and mutual induction, generator, transformer.
- AC Circuits: Alternating current, peak and r.m.s. values, reactance (inductive and capacitive), impedance, resonance in R-L-C circuits, power factor.

## 5. Modern Physics
- Structure of the Atom: Models of the atom, energy levels, emission spectra.
- Wave-particle Duality: Photoelectric effect, Einstein's photoelectric equation, work function, de Broglie wavelength.
- Radioactivity: Natural radioactivity, alpha, beta, and gamma emissions, decay equation, half-life, decay constant, nuclear fission and fusion, binding energy.
- Semiconductors: Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, p-n junction diode, rectifiers.
