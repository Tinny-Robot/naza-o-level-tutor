# Senior Secondary Composition and Grammar Companion (SS1-SS3)
## Chapter 1: Composition Types and Letter Formats
Writing compositions in WASSCE requires understanding formats, registers, and audiences.
- Narrative Essay: Telling stories with chronological order, sensory details, and clear climaxes.
- Descriptive Essay: Creating vivid mental pictures of people, places, or events.
- Argumentative Essay / Debate: Presenting balanced arguments, refuting opposing points.
- Letters: Formal, informal, and semi-formal letters. Formal letters require two addresses (writer's and recipient's), a formal date, formal salutation, a bold capitalized title, a formal body, and a formal closing ('Yours faithfully' followed by signature and full name).

## Chapter 2: Oral English and Stress
English is a stress-timed language.
- Vowels: 12 monophthongs and 8 diphthongs.
- Consonants: 24 consonant sounds including voiced and voiceless pairs.
- Word Stress: Stressed syllables in multi-syllable words. Nouns and adjectives usually have the stress on the first syllable, while verbs and prepositions have the stress on the second syllable.
