# WAEC English Language Syllabus

## 1. Comprehension & Summary
- Understanding literal and implied meanings in complex narrative, descriptive, and expository passages.
- Drawing logical inferences from facts presented in the passage.
- Identifying and replacing vocabulary in context with appropriate synonyms.
- Identifying grammatical structures (e.g., noun clauses, adjectival clauses, adverbial clauses) and their syntactic functions.
- Summarizing main arguments or key points from a passage in clear, concise, and complete sentences, eliminating redundant illustrations.

## 2. Lexis & Structure
- Concord: Subject-verb agreement, pronoun-antecedent agreement, proximity concord, and collective nouns.
- Word Classes: Nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
- Verb Tenses and Aspect: Simple present, present continuous, past perfect, future tenses, active and passive voices.
- Prepositional Phrases and Idiomatic Expressions.
- Synonyms, Antonyms, Homonyms, and vocabulary related to specific registers (e.g., science, commerce, legal, agriculture, medicine).

## 3. Essay / Composition
- Narrative Essay: Telling stories with chronological order, sensory details, and clear climaxes.
- Descriptive Essay: Creating vivid mental pictures of people, places, or events.
- Argumentative Essay / Debate: Presenting balanced arguments, refuting opposing points, and using persuasive vocabulary.
- Letters: Formal, informal, and semi-formal letters with standard layouts, greeting, and sign-offs.
- Speech Writing: Addressing specific audiences (e.g., principal, students) using appropriate salutations, rhetorical questions, and call to action.
- Article Writing: Writing for newspaper or school magazines with catchy headlines and cohesive paragraphs.
- **Marking Scheme Criteria**:
  - Content (10 Marks): Relevance to topic, address of all components.
  - Organisation (10 Marks): Paragraphing, logical sequence, opening/closing formats.
  - Expression (20 Marks): Rich vocabulary, varied sentence structures, tone control.
  - Mechanical Accuracy (10 Marks): Correct spelling, punctuation, grammar, and concord (deducting 0.5 marks for each error).

## 4. Oral English
- Vowels: 12 monophthongs (short and long vowels) and 8 diphthongs (glide vowels).
- Consonants: 24 consonant sounds including voiced and voiceless pairs, plosives, fricatives, affricates, nasals, liquids, and glides.
- Consonant Clusters: Initial and final clusters (e.g., "str-", "-pts", "-sps").
- Silent Letters: Identification of unpronounced letters in words (e.g., debt, comb, hour, receipt).
- Rhyming Words: Matching words with identical ending vowel and consonant sounds (e.g., "gate" and "wait").
- Word Stress: Stressed syllables in disyllabic, trisyllabic, and polysyllabic words, including stress shift in noun-verb pairs (e.g., EXport vs. exPORT).
- Emphatic Stress: Identifying the focus word that answers a specific query in a sentence.
