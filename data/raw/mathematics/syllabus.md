# WAEC & NECO Mathematics Syllabus

## 1. Number and Numeration
- Number Bases: Conversion from one base to another (bases 2 to 10), addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division in different bases.
- Modular Arithmetic: Concept, addition, subtraction, multiplication tables, and applications.
- Indices and Logarithms: Laws of indices, standard forms, base 10 and natural logarithms, logarithmic equations.
- Sets: Concepts, union, intersection, complement, Venn diagrams (2 and 3 sets) and applications.
- Sequences and Series: Arithmetic Progressions (AP) and Geometric Progressions (GP), n-th term, sum of terms, arithmetic and geometric means.

## 2. Algebra
- Algebraic Expressions: Factorization, expanding brackets, algebraic fractions.
- Linear Equations and Inequalities: Solving linear equations, simultaneous linear equations (graphical and algebraic methods), linear inequalities and graphs.
- Quadratic Equations: Solving by factorization, completing the square, quadratic formula, and graphing. Roots of quadratic equations.
- Simultaneous Linear & Quadratic Equations: Solution methods and graphical representation.
- Variation: Direct, inverse, joint, and partial variation, word problems.
- Matrices and Determinants: Addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication, matrix multiplication, determinant and inverse of 2x2 matrices, Cramer's rule.

## 3. Geometry and Trigonometry
- Plane Geometry: Angles at a point, parallel lines, properties of triangles and polygons, congruent and similar triangles.
- Circle Geometry: Circle theorems (angle at center, angles in same segment, angles in alternate segment, cyclic quadrilaterals, tangents to a circle).
- Trigonometry: Sine, cosine, tangent of angles, angles of elevation and depression, sine and cosine rules, graphs of trigonometric functions.
- Latitude and Longitude: Great and small circles, distance along great circles, distance along small circles, speed in knots.

## 4. Mensuration and Calculus
- Lengths, Areas, and Volumes: Perimeter and area of plane shapes (triangles, quadrilaterals, sectors, segments), surface area and volume of solids (prisms, cylinders, cones, spheres, pyramids).
- Introductory Calculus: Differentiation of polynomials ($y = ax^n$), rate of change, tangents and normals, maximum and minimum points. Integration of simple algebraic functions ($y = ax^n$, $n \neq -1$), area under a curve.
- Coordinate Geometry: Distance between two points, midpoint, gradient, equation of a straight line, parallel and perpendicular lines.

## 5. Statistics and Probability
- Data Representation: Frequency tables, bar charts, histograms, pie charts, cumulative frequency curves (ogive).
- Measures of Central Tendency and Dispersion: Mean, median, mode, range, variance, standard deviation, mean deviation (grouped and ungrouped data).
- Probability: Experimental and theoretical probability, mutually exclusive and independent events, addition and multiplication rules.
