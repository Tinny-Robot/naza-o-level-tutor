# WAEC Chemistry Syllabus

## 1. Structure of the Atom
- Subatomic Particles: Proton, neutron, electron, their relative masses and charges.
- Atomic Number, Mass Number, and Isotopes.
- Electronic Configuration: Aufbau principle, Hund's rule, Pauli exclusion principle. Shapes of s and p orbitals.

## 2. Periodic Table & Chemical Bonding
- Periodicity: Periodic trends in atomic radius, ionic radius, ionization energy, electron affinity, and electronegativity.
- Chemical Bonding: Electrovalent (ionic), covalent, coordinate (dative), metallic bonding. Intermolecular forces: Van der Waals forces, dipole-dipole, and hydrogen bonding.

## 3. Stoichiometry & Chemical Equations
- Mole Concept: Avogadro's number, molar mass, molar volume of gases (22.4 dm³ at s.t.p.).
- Formulas: Empirical and molecular formulas.
- Chemical Equations: Writing and balancing chemical and net ionic equations. Calculation of masses, moles, and gas volumes involved in chemical reactions.

## 4. States of Matter
- Kinetic Molecular Theory: Explaining gases, liquids, and solids.
- Gas Laws: Boyle's law, Charles's law, Dalton's law of partial pressures, Graham's law of diffusion. Ideal gas equation ($PV = nRT$).

## 5. Kinetics and Chemical Equilibrium
- Rates of Reaction: Definition, Collision theory, activation energy, catalyst. Factors affecting reaction rates (temperature, concentration, surface area, catalyst).
- Chemical Equilibrium: Reversible reactions, dynamic equilibrium, Le Chatelier's principle (effects of temperature, pressure, concentration).

## 6. Acids, Bases & Salts
- Theories of Acids and Bases: Arrhenius, Bronsted-Lowry, and Lewis.
- Properties: pH scale, weak and strong acids/bases, neutralization.
- Salts: Classification (acid, basic, normal, double, complex), preparation methods, solubility rules. Qualitative analysis for cations ($Fe^{2+}$, $Fe^{3+}$, $Cu^{2+}$, $Al^{3+}$, $Zn^{2+}$, $Ca^{2+}$, $NH_4^+$) and anions ($CO_3^{2-}$, $SO_4^{2-}$, $Cl^-$, $NO_3^-$).

## 7. Redox & Electrochemistry
- Redox Processes: Oxidation state, oxidizing and reducing agents, balancing redox equations.
- Electrolysis: Electrolytes, Faraday's laws of electrolysis, calculations, applications (extraction of metals, refining, electroplating). Electrochemical cells.

## 8. Chemistry of Carbon Compounds (Organic Chemistry)
- Homologous Series: Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, alkanols, alkanoic acids, esters. IUPAC nomenclature.
- Reactions: Combustion, substitution, addition, esterification, saponification, addition and condensation polymerisation.

## 9. Non-metals & Metals
- Non-metals: Preparation, properties, and uses of Hydrogen, Oxygen, Nitrogen, Carbon, Sulphur, Halogens.
- Metals: Extraction principles (Iron in blast furnace, Aluminium from bauxite, Copper). Alloys and their uses.

## 10. Environmental Chemistry
- Pollution: Air, water, and soil pollution. Greenhouse effect, acid rain, ozone layer depletion. Water treatment.
