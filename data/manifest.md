# Data Manifest: O-Level Exam-Prep Assistant

This manifest tracks the raw educational material sources, curricula, and past exams consolidated for the offline O-Level Mathematics, English Language, Physics, and Chemistry exam-prep model and vector store.

---

### WAEC (WASSCE) Syllabus
*   **Description**: Official curriculum standards and exam breakdown for Mathematics, English Language, Physics, and Chemistry.
*   **Source URL**: [WAEC e-Learning Portal](https://waeconline.org.ng/e-learning)
*   **Access Date**: July 17, 2026
*   **Copyright / Licensing Notes**: Public exam syllabus outline published by the West African Examinations Council (WAEC). Distributed freely for educational and study purposes.

### NERDC Senior Secondary Curriculum
*   **Description**: Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council national senior secondary school curriculum guidelines.
*   **Source URL**: [NERDC Portal](https://nerdc.org.ng/)
*   **Access Date**: July 17, 2026
*   **Copyright / Licensing Notes**: Federal Government of Nigeria curriculum outlines, made publicly accessible for alignment of educational materials.

## 2. Past Questions & Solutions

### ALOC Endpoints Database
*   **Description**: An open-source API providing structured questions and responses for Nigerian unified examinations (UTME, WASSCE, NECO).
*   **Source URL**: [Seunope/aloc-endpoints on GitHub](https://github.com/Seunope/aloc-endpoints)
*   **Access Date**: July 17, 2026
*   **Copyright / Licensing Notes**: MIT Licensed. Contains community-sourced past question aggregates. Answers and explanations are cross-checked programmatically.

### MySchoolGist
*   **Description**: Educational portal aggregating WAEC/NECO past questions, alternative-to-practical manuals, and marking guides.
*   **Source URL**: [MySchoolGist WASSCE Past Questions](https://myschoolgist.com)
*   **Access Date**: July 17, 2026
*   **Copyright / Licensing Notes**: Copyrighted by MySchoolGist; used here solely as a public educational reference to cross-verify question prompts and worked solution structures.

### SchoolNgr
*   **Description**: Unified CBT portal and syllabus-aligned questions aggregator.
*   **Source URL**: [SchoolNgr Physics / Chemistry](https://schoolngr.com)
*   **Access Date**: July 17, 2026
*   **Copyright / Licensing Notes**: Public study resource; used for cross-referencing and checking step-by-step math derivations in Physics theory papers.

## 4. Usage Rights & Red Flags

> [!WARNING]
> While syllabus guidelines and past question prompts are freely available for candidate study, compiling and distributing raw proprietary textbooks or wholesale commercial databases is restricted. The vector chunks and training pairs in this dataset have been synthesized, cross-verified, and rewritten to avoid direct copying of commercial answer keys or copyright-protected educational material.
